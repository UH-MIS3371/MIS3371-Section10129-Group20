Cancelled is reachable from any non-final state (Submitted, Under Review, Incomplete, or Accepted) when the provider or coordinator withdraws the referral with a reason. Rejected, Cancelled, and Completed are final states.

State definitions

StateMeaningSet by
SubmittedReferral passed field validation and the duplicate check, and received a referral ID.System
IncompleteMissing required information, or the receiving department asked for more information; returned to the provider.System / Receiving Department
Under ReviewRouted to the receiving department and waiting on a decision.System
AcceptedReceiving department approved the referral for scheduling.Receiving Department
RejectedReceiving department declined the referral; a reason is recorded and visible to the provider. Final.Receiving Department
ScheduledPatient has an appointment booked for the service.Scheduling Staff
CompletedPatient received the service; the referral is closed. Final.Scheduling Staff
CancelledWithdrawn by the provider or coordinator before completion, with a reason recorded. Final.Referring Provider / Referral Coordinator
