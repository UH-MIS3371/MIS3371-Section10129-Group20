Transaction Workflow: Submit a Patient Referral

MIS 3371  |  Week 3 Capstone Design Checkpoint  |  Team TPS_3371_TEAM_20  |  Patient Referral Management System

A referring provider submits a referral for a patient to receive a specific service. The system checks the referral, routes it to the right department, and tracks it until the patient is scheduled or the referral is closed.

Workflow diagram

1. Referring provider submits a referral (patient ID, reason, requested service, urgency).
↓
2. System checks that all required fields are present.
Exception path
A field is missing → status becomes Incomplete, and the referral is returned to the provider with the missing fields listed.
↓
3. System checks for an existing active referral for the same patient and service.
Exception path
A duplicate is found → the submission is blocked and the existing referral ID is shown to the provider.
↓
4. System assigns a referral ID and sets the status to Submitted.
↓
5. System routes the referral to the department that offers the requested service; status becomes Under Review.
↓
6. Receiving department reviews the referral and makes a decision.
Branches
Accept → status becomes Accepted.
Reject with a reason → status becomes Rejected (exception path).
Request more information → status becomes Incomplete and returns to the provider (exception path).
↓
7. Scheduling staff mark the referral Scheduled, then Completed once the patient receives the service.
