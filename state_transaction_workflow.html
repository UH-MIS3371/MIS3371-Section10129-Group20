State Transition Diagram: Patient Referral

MIS 3371  |  Week 3 Capstone Design Checkpoint  |  Team TPS_3371_TEAM_20  |  Patient Referral Management System

A new referral that is missing a required field goes straight to Incomplete. Cancelled is reachable from any non-final state (Submitted, Under Review, Incomplete, or Accepted) when the provider or coordinator withdraws the referral with a reason. Rejected, Cancelled, and Completed are final states.
